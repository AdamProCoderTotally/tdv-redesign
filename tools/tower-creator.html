MY TOWERS

Create or select a tower.

EXPORT

1. EXPORT GAME CODE → 2. Copy → 3. Paste into the CUSTOM TOWERS & ENEMIES — PASTE ZONE in js/config.js → 4. Launch the game. Your tower appears in the shop and works everywhere.

MY ENEMIES

Create or select an enemy.

NOTES

Enemies with BOSS enabled get a shield bar, fanfare and 1.5× gold, and can be picked as wave bosses. Movement type copies built-in behaviours (splitting molten, digging tunneller, boss sword swings...). Exported enemies join the WAVES & MODE tab dropdowns automatically.

CUSTOM MODE

A custom mode is how players play your maps and enemies. It appears in the mode menu once exported.

WAVES

EXPORT

Paste into the CUSTOM MODES & WAVES — PASTE ZONE at the bottom of js/config.js.
